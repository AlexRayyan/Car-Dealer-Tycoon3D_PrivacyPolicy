Privacy Policy

Car Dealer Simulation
Last updated: December 2025

ReemX Studios built Car Dealer Simulation as a free-to-play mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The game may automatically collect non-personal information, including:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and app version
  • Gameplay activity and progress
  • Session duration and usage statistics
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Advertising ID (for ad display and measurement)
2. How We Use the Information
  • Improve gameplay experience
  • Analyze game performance and usage
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Display advertisements
  • Process in-app purchases via Google Play
3. Third-Party Services

The game uses third-party services that may collect information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Google Play Billing (for in-app purchases)
4. Advertisements

The game displays third-party advertisements. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized and may use the device Advertising ID.

You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings.

5. Analytics

Analytics tools are used to understand how players interact with the game. The data collected is aggregated and anonymous and used only to improve performance and user experience.

6. In-App Purchases

Car Dealer Simulation offers optional in-app purchases. All purchases are processed securely through Google Play.

We do not collect or store payment information such as credit or debit card details.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Information may be shared only with required service providers or when legally required.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information; however, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

This game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you believe a child has shared personal information, please contact us immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
