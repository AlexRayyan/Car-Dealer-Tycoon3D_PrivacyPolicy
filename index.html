ReemX Studios built Car Dealer Tycoon as a free-to-play mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our game.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information (via Google Login)

  • Name (from Google account)
  • Email address
  • User ID

b) Non-Personal Information

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and app version
  • Gameplay activity, progress, and in-game statistics
  • Session duration and usage behavior
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Advertising ID

c) Data Stored via Firebase

  • Game progress and player data (Firestore)
  • Analytics data (Firebase Analytics)
  • Remote configuration data (Firebase Remote Config)
2. How We Use the Information
  • To create and manage your game account
  • To save and sync your game progress
  • To personalize gameplay experience
  • To analyze performance and improve the game
  • To fix bugs and technical issues
  • To display ads and measure ad performance
  • To process in-app purchases via Google Play
3. Third-Party Services

The game uses third-party services that may collect and process data:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Sign-In (Authentication)
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Google Play Billing
  • LevelPlay (IronSource Mediation)
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads

These services may collect information according to their respective privacy policies.

4. Advertisements

The game displays third-party advertisements using mediation platforms (LevelPlay). Ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

  • Uses Advertising ID
  • May track ad interactions and impressions

You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings.

5. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the game. This data helps us improve gameplay, balance features, and fix issues.

Analytics data is aggregated and does not directly identify users.

6. In-App Purchases

Car Dealer Tycoon offers optional in-app purchases. All transactions are securely processed by Google Play.

We do not collect or store payment information such as credit/debit card details.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Data may be shared with trusted service providers (such as Firebase and ad networks) only for functionality, analytics, and monetization purposes, or when required by law.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

This game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights
  • You can request deletion of your data
  • You can stop using the app at any time
  • You can manage ad personalization in device settings
11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
